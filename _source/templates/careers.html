How we work: contract per project

RUNHTec engages professionals as independent contractors for each project, not as salaried employees. When a project matches your skills, we offer you a written engagement for that project, with the scope of work, your fee and the dates agreed before you start. You are paid for that project, and each new project is agreed separately.

  1. Apply once with your skills and experience.
  2. We review and may call you for a short interview or practical test.
  3. Join our register of approved contractors.
  4. Get project offers with a clear scope, fee and dates.
