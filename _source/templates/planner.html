How the planner calculates
  • Peak load assumes everything in the list can run at the same time. This is the safe, worst-case figure.
  • Inverter: peak load plus 25% headroom, and enough surge capacity for your largest motor (pump, fridge, AC) to start, at about 3 times its running power. Rounded up to a standard size.
  • Battery: energy each appliance uses during the outage (up to its daily hours), divided by 92% inverter efficiency and 90% usable depth of discharge for lithium (LiFePO₄). Shown in 5.12 kWh modules, a common 48 V size.
  • Solar: daily energy divided by peak sun hours (4.5 for Rwanda, 5.0 for Uganda) and a 75% system efficiency for heat, dust, wiring and battery losses. Shown in 550 W panels.
  • Generator: peak load plus 25% headroom at 0.8 power factor, checked against motor starting, then rounded up to a standard kVA size. Fuel use follows a standard diesel fuel curve (0.08 L/h per rated kW plus 0.25 L per kWh produced) at the average outage load.
