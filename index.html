Why RUNHTec

One partner from first site visit to long-term support.

RUNHTec Contractors Ltd provides integrated technical solutions for residential, commercial and industrial applications. We aim to deliver practical engineering that is coordinated, maintainable and appropriate to each client's operating environment.

  • Full project lifecycle from requirements and site assessment to installation, testing, commissioning and maintenance.
  • Coordinated disciplines. Where a project needs several services, we manage them under one project approach.
  • Safety and quality through safe working practices, appropriate materials, technical checks and clear communication.
  • Regional presence with a head office in Kigali and a branch in Kampala.

How we work

A practical sequence from first requirement to handover and support.

  1. 01

    Consultation

    Understand your needs, scope and expected outcome.

  2. 02

    Site assessment

    Review existing conditions, measurements, access, safety and technical constraints.

  3. 03

    Planning & design

    Develop the appropriate technical solution, materials and work plan.

  4. 04

    Installation
