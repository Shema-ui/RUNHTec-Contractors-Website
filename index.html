About RUNHTec

One partner, from first site visit to long-term support.

RUNHTec Contractors Ltd is an engineering and construction company providing integrated technical solutions for residential, commercial and industrial applications. We bring together electrical, energy, mechanical, plumbing, construction and related technical systems.

Working across Rwanda and Uganda, we support clients through the whole project lifecycle: understanding requirements, site assessment, installation, testing, commissioning and maintenance. Where a project needs several disciplines, we coordinate them under one project approach.
