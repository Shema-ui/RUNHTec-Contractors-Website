About us

Practical engineering for the way buildings really work.

RUNHTec Contractors Ltd is an engineering and technical services company working across Rwanda and Uganda.

Company overview

Integrated technical solutions under one project approach.

RUNHTec Contractors Ltd provides integrated technical solutions for residential, commercial and industrial applications. Our service offering brings together electrical, energy, mechanical, refrigeration, security, connectivity and construction-related technical systems.

We support clients through the full project lifecycle, from understanding requirements and site assessment to installation, testing, commissioning and maintenance.

Our positioning

We aim to deliver practical engineering solutions that are coordinated, maintainable and appropriate to each client's operating environment. Where projects require multiple disciplines, RUNHTec can coordinate complementary technical services under one project approach.
